Field Library

Learn to identify symptoms and read biological indicators for Sri Lankan coconut crops.

Healthy Fronds Control
Cocos nucifera

Healthy green leaf tissue indicates optimal nitrogen and magnesium absorption and robust photosynthetic function. Standard baseline for checking crop health indices.

Flat, rigid leaflets Even dark green color Intact margins and petioles
Caterpillar Presence CCI Infestation
Opisina arenosella (Larvae)

Active coconut caterpillars feeding on leaflet tissues. They construct protective web tunnels made of silk and feces on the undersides of leaflets, hiding from sunlight.

Greenish-brown larvae White/grey silken tunnels Scraped brown feeding tracks
Caterpillar Leaflet Damage CCI Damage
Opisina arenosella (Scorched Sign)

Advanced leaflet damage signature caused by larvae feeding. Leaves lose moisture, skeletonize, turn completely greyish-brown, and curl up. Fronds get a burnt appearance.

Scorched brown appearance Skeletonized leaf structure Papery, fragile dead leaflets
WCLWD Leaflet Yellowing WCLWD Yellowing
Phytoplasma (Chlorosis Vector)

Characterized by intense chlorosis (yellowing) starting at the leaflet tips and extending inwards. WCLWD-associated yellowing typically starts on middle-tier fronds.

Tip-to-base yellowing Affects middle and lower fronds Accompanied by leaflet flaccidity
WCLWD Leaflet Flaccidity WCLWD Drooping
Phytoplasma (Droop Sign)

Loss of cell turgor pressure caused by Phytoplasma vascular disruption. Leaflets fold along the midrib and droop downwards, resembling a rib-cage structure.

Saucer-like curved leaflets Bending along vertical axis Loss of leaflet rigidity
WCLWD Advanced Drying WCLWD Drying
Phytoplasma (Vascular Collapse)

Final stages of Leaf Wilt disease where tissues die off completely. The stalks weaken at the petiole attachment point, causing the whole frond to snap and hang down.

Snapped petioles Hanging dead leaves at trunk Severe crop canopy drop

Offline Distribution Guide

Cocoshield AI runs entirely in your browser — no internet, no server, no installation. Just copy the folder and open index.html.

1 The Complete File Bundle

📦
Everything is self-contained in one folder The entire application — including the TensorFlow.js AI library and all MobileNetV2 model weights — is bundled inside the coconut_web_model folder. No internet connection is required at any point. No software needs to be installed.
📋
Copy the entire folder — never move files individually Select the complete coconut_web_model folder and copy it to any location — USB drive, SD card, laptop, or desktop. The folder can be placed anywhere on any drive letter or path. All file references are relative, so the location does not matter.
📁 coconut_web_model/
├── index.html (Main application — double-click this)
├── app.js (AI engine & logic)
├── tf.min.js (TensorFlow.js library — bundled locally)
├── model-data.js (MobileNetV2 weights — embedded as data)
├── style.css (Visual design)
├── sw.js (Service worker for http:// mode)
├── manifest.json (PWA configuration)
├── icon-192.png
├── icon-512.png
└── 📁 kaggle/working/tfjs_model/
    ├── model.json (Network graph definition)
    ├── group1-shard1of3.bin (Weights ~4MB)
    ├── group1-shard2of3.bin (Weights ~4MB)
    └── group1-shard3of3.bin (Weights ~0.7MB)

2 USB Drive / SD Card Distribution

💾
Insert a USB drive or SD card Use any USB flash drive or micro SD card with at least 25 MB of free space. Format to FAT32 or NTFS for maximum device compatibility.
📤
Copy the entire project folder Paste the complete coconut_web_model folder onto the USB or SD card. Do not rename the folder or move files out of it — all paths are relative to the folder root.
🌴
Distribute to farmers and agricultural officers Hand the USB drive to field workers. On the target device, open the coconut_web_model folder and double-click index.html. The AI diagnostic system starts immediately — no setup, no installation, no internet needed.
💡 The total application size is approximately 25 MB — including the bundled TensorFlow.js library, embedded model weights, and all application files. It fits on any modern USB drive or SD card.

3 Running Without Internet

🖱️
Double-click index.html — that's it Open the coconut_web_model folder and double-click index.html. The app opens in your browser with all features fully working: AI diagnosis, image upload, and live camera capture. No Python, no server, no internet connection required at any stage.
📱
On Android / Mobile Devices Connect the USB drive via an OTG adapter, copy the coconut_web_model folder to internal storage, then open index.html using a file manager that can launch it in Chrome. All AI inference and camera features work directly.
✅ No server required. The TensorFlow.js library (tf.min.js) and all MobileNetV2 model weights (model-data.js) are bundled locally inside the folder. The browser loads them directly — no network requests are made at any point during diagnosis.

4 Updating the Application

📥
Manual update for USB-distributed copies Download the latest version of the coconut_web_model folder from the project source. Replace all files on the USB drive. Distribute the updated USB to field workers.
✅ Once cached by the Service Worker, the app works completely offline — including AI inference, camera capture, and all diagnostic features. No data is ever sent to external servers.
